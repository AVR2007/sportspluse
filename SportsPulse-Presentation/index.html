PROBLEM STATEMENT & OBJECTIVE

Sports data is big. Tools that explain it are black boxes.

Coaches and analysts search messy match reports, pick lineups under budgets, and plan training slots by hand. Typos break searches and no tool shows why a result is optimal. Students rarely see algorithms solving a real problem.

Target usersCoaches, analysts and faculty who want sports answers and algorithm proof.
Goal 1: Search and mineTypo-proof search and phrase mining over match reports.

Goal 2: Plan and optimiseBest lineup, salary cap, training flow, selection conflicts.

Goal 3: Prove it26 algorithms, 202 tests, and a benchmark lab with CSV export.
